The Art of Modern Tailoring: A Deep Dive into SS24 Trends

By Audrey Hepburn | Published on August 15, 2024 | 5 min read

In the ever-evolving landscape of fashion, tailoring remains a timeless art form. The Spring/Summer 2024 season marks a significant shift, moving beyond the traditional confines of suiting to embrace a more fluid, expressive, and versatile approach. This season is not just about impeccably cut blazers and trousers; it's about redefining what power dressing means in a modern context.

Designers are exploring deconstructed silhouettes, unexpected fabric combinations, and a softer, more relaxed aesthetic. We're seeing oversized blazers paired with flowing skirts, sharp-shouldered jackets in pastel hues, and trousers that range from wide-leg culottes to slim, cropped styles. The focus is on personal expression and comfort without sacrificing an ounce of sophistication.

"Fashion is not something that exists in dresses only. Fashion is in the sky, in the street, fashion has to do with ideas, the way we live, what is happening." — Coco Chanel

The New Palette: Earthy Neutrals and Bold Accents

While classic navy, charcoal, and black remain staples, SS24 introduces a palette inspired by nature. Think sandy beiges, olive greens, and terracotta, often punctuated with a surprising pop of color like cerulean blue or vibrant coral. This blend of earthy tones and bold accents creates a look that is both grounded and exciting, perfect for transitioning from the boardroom to a social evening.
